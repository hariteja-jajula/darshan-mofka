Init and Finalize are near-constant fixed costs (~0.2–0.3 s init; finalize scales with in-flight backlog at shutdown). Push is the only phase that scales with I/O volume — and it stays cheap unless each push rides a synchronous I/O step (mpi). Total streaming overhead = streaming wall − baseline wall.
